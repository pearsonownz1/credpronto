You should be able to read the translation before you spend money on it.

Upload a PDF or phone photos. CredPronto prepares the translation so you can review it next to your original. The preview is free. If it is right, you pay the price shown up front, starting at $9.95 per page, and receive the certified PDF.

## Before you upload

- Include every page, including stamps and the back of the document when it has text
- Use a clear photo or a text-based PDF
- Check names and dates in the preview before you pay
