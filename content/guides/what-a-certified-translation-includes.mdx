A certified translation is the English version of a foreign-language document plus a certification statement. The statement says the translation is complete and accurate.

CredPronto includes that certification with the finished PDF. Pricing starts at $9.95 per page. You can preview the translation before you pay.

## What you receive

- A full translation of the pages you upload
- A certification statement with the delivery
- A preview you can read before you purchase

Upload a PDF or clear phone photos of every page. Include stamps, seals, and the back of a page when it has text.
