A clear upload is the most reliable way to get a certified translation you can check yourself.

## Accepted files

Upload a PDF, or take phone photos of each page. Photos should show the whole page, with the text large enough to read.

## What to include

Include every page that has text, seals, or stamps. If a page is double-sided, photograph both sides. Keep the pages in order.

## What happens next

You preview the finished translation for free. Certified translations start at $9.95 per page, and you pay only if the preview is right.
